# Conversion Patterns — Home Improvement / Contractor Lead-Gen

Per website-builder skill's Local Service CRO Rules — applied directly to this build.

## CTAs
- Primary: "Get a Free Quote" / "Call Now" — phone number visible on button, not hidden behind it
- Above-fold headline format: "[Service] Done Right" / "[City/Region]'s Trusted Tract-Home Contractor" style — avoid generic "Your Trusted Partner"
- Sticky phone number in header (desktop) and sticky call/quote bar (mobile)

## Trust signals for this niche
- Licensed & insured (all states CCA operates in) + license number
- Years in business / homes renovated count
- Free, no-obligation estimate — no pressure framing
- Financing available badge — required per skill for high-ticket services ($5K+): foundation repair, home additions, whole-home renovation, ADU builds
- Permit-handled-for-you messaging (directly answers PAA #13 "do I need permission")

## Social proof
- Real photos over stock: job-site photos, branded-shirt crew, before/after pairs — NO smiling-family stock photography
- Before/after photo pairs are the dominant trust device in this niche (stronger signal than star counts alone per competitor audit)
- Reviews/proof repeated at every scroll depth: hero trust bar, mid-page, and again beside the quote form

## Form design
- Max 4 fields: Name, Phone, Service needed (dropdown of the 7 services), Address/ZIP
- No email requirement, no "how did you hear about us," no comments box — qualifying happens on the callback
- AEO hidden fields (traffic_source, landing_url) per build template §6b — invisible, no UX impact

## Funnel
Land (service-specific or homepage) → see phone + trust bar immediately → scroll through proof (before/afters, credentials) → FAQ answers objections (cost, permits, timeline) → quote form with proof repeated beside it → confirmation.
