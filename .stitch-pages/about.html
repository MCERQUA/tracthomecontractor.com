About Tract Home Contractor

Former production-home builders turned independent contractors, bringing master-planned quality to your custom renovations.

Our Heritage

Built on the Foundation of Production Excellence.

For over a decade, we managed the construction of hundreds of homes in major master-planned communities. We learned the exact science of scheduling, the critical importance of standardized quality, and how to manage complex trades efficiently.

We transitioned to independent contracting to bring that same systematic rigor to individual home renovations, additions, and structural repairs. We don't just build; we engineer solutions tailored to the specific quirks of tract-built homes.
