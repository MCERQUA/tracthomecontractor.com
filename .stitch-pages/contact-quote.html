Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our quoting and estimation process.

Is the on-site estimate really free? expand_more
Yes. We provide a complimentary on-site assessment for homeowners within our service area. This allows us to provide an accurate written proposal rather than a rough guess over the phone.
Do you help with HOA approvals? expand_more
Absolutely. Since we specialize in tract homes and subdivisions, we are very familiar with HOA architectural review processes. We provide the necessary drawings and material samples required for submission.
How long is my quote valid for? expand_more
Our written proposals are typically valid for 30 days. Due to fluctuating material costs, especially for lumber and concrete, prices may need to be adjusted if you decide to proceed after the 30-day window.
